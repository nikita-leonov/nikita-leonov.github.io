Face-off Studio — Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 14, 2026

Face-off Studio is a personal, self-hosted application operated by Nikita Leonov that processes the operator's own recreational hockey game recordings and publishes short highlight clips to the operator's own social media accounts (YouTube, Instagram, TikTok).

What data the application handles

The application handles only content and account credentials belonging to its operator: game video recordings the operator has the right to use, and OAuth tokens for the operator's own social media accounts. It has no user base beyond its operator, no sign-up, and collects no personal information from any third party.

TikTok integration

When the operator connects a TikTok account, the application receives OAuth tokens scoped to basic profile information (user.info.basic) and video upload (video.upload). These tokens are used exclusively to upload the operator's own video clips to the operator's own TikTok account, at the operator's direction. Tokens are stored locally on the operator's machine and are never transmitted to any party other than TikTok's API endpoints.

Data sharing and retention

No data is sold, shared, or disclosed to third parties. No analytics or tracking is performed. OAuth tokens are retained locally until the operator disconnects the account, at which point they are deleted. Uploaded videos are governed by the respective platform's terms once published.
